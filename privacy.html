Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

Thank you for choosing PDFlux. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

PDFlux is built with a privacy-first approach. All document processing, including PDF parsing, optical character recognition (OCR), layout analysis, and EPUB generation, happens strictly on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your documents, images, or extracted text to any external cloud servers.

2. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
